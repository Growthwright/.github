## Keep your tools. Cut the Tab Tax.

Small marketing teams pay the **Tab Tax** every day: switching between tools, relearning them when they change, copying data by hand and double-checking what each tool and its AI produced.

Growthwright doesn't add another tool or cut the ones you rely on. We connect the tools your team already pays for to the AI it already uses (Claude, ChatGPT or whichever you've picked), move your workflows over in short working sessions, and coach two people on your team to run them without us.

### How it works: The 90-Day Build
1. **Find the tax.** Watch how the work actually gets done and measure the hours lost.
2. **Connect and run.** Move your priority workflows onto your own AI tools, one session at a time, using real work.
3. **Hand it over.** Each workflow comes with a cheat sheet, and your people run it without us. We measure the tax again at handover.

Three months, or a one-month fast track. Optional monthly coaching after handover.
